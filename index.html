Job agent

Find jobs that fit you

Pick a profile: tech, security, care or NHS sponsored. The agent searches Reed.co.uk and Adzuna, scores each job for that profile, and flags anything that could rule you out.

Jobs from Reed.co.uk and Jobs by Adzuna. Scores use the job title and summary only, so always read the full advert before you apply. Sponsorship labels come from the advert text and the UK register of licensed sponsors, so confirm with the employer before relying on them. For Care and NHS, the agent also reads the full Reed advert for the top 30 jobs.
